Works best with clean, computer-made PDFs showing a single melody. It reads note heads, note lengths (including dotted notes and triplets), and the top note of stacked chords. It does not read rests, sharps/flats written beside a note, ties, or repeats and endings (it plays straight through). Fix those in the list.
